## Interpretation and limits

- 215 question attempts: 183 correct, 32 incorrect, no skips; raw pooled accuracy 85.1%. Repeated concepts/questions are not independent observations of mastery.
- 13 of the incorrect answers were marked Confident; 7 Educated guess; 2 Unsure; 10 had no confidence recorded.
- 26 correct answers were low-confidence: 18 Educated guess and 8 Unsure. Together with the 32 misses, the review queue is 58 attempts.
- The latest supplied practice exam had 55/65 correct (84.6% raw), 5 confident misses, and 16 uncertain correct answers. Its displayed 781 is a scaled score, not a raw percentage.
- Small standalone domain samples and absent mixed-set domain breakdowns do not establish precise domain mastery percentages. Topic groupings below are editorial classifications.
- Multi-select: 36/43 correct; single-choice: 147/172 correct. The data does not support treating multi-select format itself as the central problem.
- Timing summaries show 33m49s on the practice exam and 24m21s on the pretest. They show no demonstrated time pressure; they do not prove rushing caused an individual error.
