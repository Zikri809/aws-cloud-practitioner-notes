# Study desk design

Direction selected by the owner: dark layout with orange accents. Main task: make the existing Cloud Practitioner notes easy and quick to study.

Reading this as a certification study workspace for self-paced learners, using a dark technical handbook language. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Color: ink canvas #101419, graphite surface #181e25, raised surface #202833, pale text #edf1f5, secondary text #aab5c3, orange accent #ffad5c. Orange identifies the current topic, progress, and primary action.
- Typography: system Segoe UI on Windows and system sans elsewhere, for clear long-form reading without font downloads. Strong compact headings establish structure; code alone uses monospace.
- Layout: persistent left contents navigation; generous central reading column; contextual right contents rail on large screens. Mobile uses a labeled menu and a native dialog for search.
- Spacing: 8px base with greater separation between chapter groups than between items, allowing scanning without stretching the reading length.
- Hierarchy: one outlined continue-reading panel, a compact chapter list, and a quieter exam rail. Cards group actual study tasks; chapter rows reflect a sequence rather than marketing features.
- Identity: orange chapter markers and a repeated vertical reading-progress line connect the outline to the active chapter. Product name is text, with no generated logo or illustration.
- Icons: book, bookmark, search, list, check, and directional chevrons indicate their corresponding actions. Lucide is used for these functional symbols only.
- Motion: short color and border transitions acknowledge input. No decorative animation, gradients, or background patterns; reduced-motion settings are respected.
- Evidence: counts derive from source files; reading times are estimates at 220 words per minute. Original notes and images retain attribution. The older study guide is explicitly labeled as CLF-C01 material.
