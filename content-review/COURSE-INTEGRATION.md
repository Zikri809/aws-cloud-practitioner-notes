Updated 20 October 2026 from the owner's local `AWS Study` collection. Course capture date: 16 October 2026. Source: [AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials](https://www.coursera.org/learn/aws-cloud-practitioner-essentials), delivered by AWS on Coursera.

## Source boundaries

The module `transcripts/` and `readings/` directories preserve course text. Each module's `sources/Source-Index.md` records lesson URLs; `sources/course-extraction.json` and `sources/Verification.md` record capture and integrity checks. The module guides, quick revision sheets, flashcards, and original practice questions are reconstructed study aids with editorial extensions. They are not themselves official AWS publications.

The separate `Exam Domain Focus` collection also includes editorial syllabus expansions and a personal AWS Skill Builder assessment review. It cannot be described as wholly Coursera course content. Personal scores, assessment identifiers, question numbers, answer selections, and assessment wording have not been imported into the public site.

This update integrates the reconstructed study explanations rather than publishing preserved transcripts or copying complete course readings. Course-background links identify the relevant module; AWS documentation supports technical qualifications. These background links are not claims that the introductory lesson contains every editorial explanation. The section map records exact local study sources. Existing lifecycle corrections remain in force. This is a content integration, not a new factual verification of every historical chapter or practice question.

## Full detailed integration

All 19 exam tasks now contribute their detailed explanations to all 19 website chapters: 170 substantive sections, including worked scenarios, service comparisons, corrections, and recall prompts. The original task reading-route lists and private review navigation are omitted. Sections spanning compute, storage, monitoring, governance, and analytics are distributed to their matching chapters, with links connecting the parts of each task.

The generated `course-notes/` files contain roughly 44,000 words. The reader places this material before each chapter's existing reference notes. Search and estimated reading times use the combined chapter source. The study guide includes all 19 task starting points as well as the thirteen-module course map.

`course-section-map.json` records the originating task file, SHA-256 source hash, section heading, and destination chapter for each of the 170 sections. `scripts/import-course-notes.mjs` regenerates the committed material from the external study archive. The archive is only needed for regeneration, not builds or deployment.

Private assessment navigation is removed, assessment-specific phrasing is adapted into general concepts, and course-guide links are replaced with public module-background links. Revision/flashcard pointers become reference-note links; practice pointers explicitly lead to community sets rather than implying that official or local assessment questions were imported. Course captures, reconstructed explanations, and editorial extensions remain distinguished.
